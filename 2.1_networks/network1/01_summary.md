## 📘 1. Summary of the Task
Describe the purpose of this activity in your own words:  
- What is the goal of the lab?  
- What network are you expected to create?


The Activity aims to have an understanding of how a computer talks to the other computers on the network. The Objective of the lab is to establish a small LAN (Local Area Network) with a switch and 5 PCs. We are asked to connect all 5 PCs to the switch and set its IP and test by sending ping to the IP of 5 others.
