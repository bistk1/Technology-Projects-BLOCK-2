
## 📘 1. Objective (In Your Own Words)
Briefly describe the purpose of this lab.  
What were you trying to achieve?
---

In this lab, I will develop the ability to create and oversee computer networks using a server and its associated client devices. The main focus of this task is to configure the server, link all of the client devices, and ensure proper communication among all of the devices in the network. During the completion of this task, I was also tasked with researching and analyzing different server services to learn and master methods for properly testing network connections.
