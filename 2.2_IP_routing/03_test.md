## 3.3 Interpretation
Explain what the ping results mean and what errors might look like.

The ping results show whether two devices can communicate with each other over a network. Successful ping results display replies from the destination device, showing that the connection is working.

If the ping fails, errors such as "Request timed out", "Destination host unreachable" or "Unknown host" may appear. These errors can indicate network connection problems, incorrect IP settings or DNS configuration issues.
