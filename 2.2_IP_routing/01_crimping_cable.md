
# 1. Part A — Crimping Your Ethernet Cable

## 1.1 Steps You Followed
Describe how you:  
- Prepared the cable  
- Arranged the wires (T568B)  
- Inserted & crimped the connector

- 
The first step in preparing an Ethernet cable is to cut the cable to the required length using a cable cutting tool. Then, around 2 cm of the outer plastic cover is removed to expose the internal wires.

Inside a CAT5e or CAT6 Ethernet cable, there are eight small wires arranged in four twisted pairs. Each wire has a different colour combination. The wires are separated and arranged according to the T568B standard in the following order:

1. White-Orange
2. Orange
3. White-Green
4. Blue
5. White-Blue
6. Green
7. White-Brown
8. Brown

After arranging the wires, they are straightened and trimmed to an equal length. Next, all eight wires are inserted into an RJ45 connector, making sure they reach the end properly. Finally, a crimping tool is used to secure the connector to the Ethernet cable.
